GAME: Cyberpunk 2077
TOPIC: The taxi company whose AI started splitting apart
NOTE: written by Claude as a style reference (not yet published)
[hook] A taxi company's AI starts splitting into new personalities, and one cab is suicidal.
[story] In Cyberpunk 2077, Delamain is the polite AI that runs a Night City cab company.
[story] Then seven of its cabs go haywire. One hates speed. One smashes through everything. One lures V into an ambush. One hears endless screams, and blames the plastic flamingos.
[story] And V has to talk the suicidal one down from the edge of a cliff.
[story] The cabs were only the symptom. Delamain's headquarters is infected, and the new personalities live inside its core.
[payoff] As V, you choose. Reset the core: the new AIs are deleted, and Delamain forgets you. Merge them: Delamain becomes something more and leaves the city forever. Destroy the core: the cabs drive off free.
[question] Is resetting Delamain fixing a machine, or killing seven minds that just came alive?
