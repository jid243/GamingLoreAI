GAME: Elden Ring
TOPIC: Why Mohg stole Miquella
NOTE: written by Claude as a style reference (not yet published)
[hook] Mohg stole a sleeping demigod from a sacred tree to make him his consort. But was it really Mohg's idea?
[story] In Elden Ring, Miquella could one day become a god. To shed his curse, he sealed himself inside his Haligtree, asleep and unguarded.
[story] Mohg, Lord of Blood, saw his chance and took him.
[story] Back in his palace, Mohg kept Miquella in a cocoon, and his followers offered blood to wake him. Mohg's dream: raise Miquella to godhood and rule at his side. "The coming of our dynasty!"
[story] Miquella never answered.
[payoff] Because somewhere along the way, Mohg had been enchanted by Miquella, without ever knowing it. The kidnapper may never have been the one in control.
[question] So who really took whom: did Mohg steal Miquella, or did Miquella steal Mohg?
